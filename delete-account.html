Delete your Gambish account

Last updated: November 2026

Option 1 – in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Gambish and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

For your security, if you signed in a while ago the app may ask you to sign out and sign in again before it will delete your account.

Option 2 – by email

If you cannot open the app, email support@gambishapp.com from the email address registered on your Gambish account with the subject “Delete my Gambish account”. We may ask you to confirm it is you. We will delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in
  • Your private data (notification token, saved payment number)
  • All your listings, with their photos and videos
  • Your notifications, favourites, follows, followers and blocks
  • Reviews you wrote
  • The conversations you took part in, including voice messages and photos. The other person will no longer see these conversations.

What we may keep

  • Payment records for paid features, because the law requires us to keep accounting records.
  • Reports and safety records, and identity verification records (if you applied for the Verified badge), for as long as needed to protect users, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. These are kept privately and are not shown to anyone else. You can ask us to delete them earlier, and we will unless a safety or legal reason applies.

Deletion cannot be undone. See our Privacy Policy for details.
