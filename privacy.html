Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Gambish (“we”, “us”) is a marketplace app where people in The Gambia buy and sell items, vehicles, property and services. It is operated by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME], [YOUR ADDRESS], The Gambia. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and the choices you have. By using Gambish you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address, a username, and your password. Your password is handled by Google’s Firebase Authentication – we cannot see it.
  • Profile: a profile photo, a short bio, the place you are based (text you type), and a website, if you add them.
  • Listings: the title, description, price, category, location text, photos and videos you post.
  • Messages: the text, photos and voice messages you send to other users in chat, and whether messages were delivered or read.
  • Activity: your favourites, the people you follow or block, reviews and ratings you give, and reports you make.
  • Identity verification (optional): if you apply for the “Verified” badge, a photo of your National ID card or passport (front, and back for an ID card) and the document type.
  • Payments: when you buy a paid feature such as a Boost, we record the plan, amount, status and the payment provider’s reference. We never see your mobile-money PIN or card details. If you switch on auto-renew, we also keep the mobile-money number and provider you chose; the full number is stored in a private area that other users cannot read, and the app only displays the last four digits.
  • Phone number: not required today. If we later ask you to verify a phone number, the app will tell you, and we will store it privately.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, app version and language.
  • Crash reports and usage events: to find bugs and understand what works, release versions of the app send crash reports and simple usage events (for example “signed up”, “posted a listing”, “bought a Boost”), linked to an internal user ID – not your name, email or phone number.
  • Notification token: a code that lets us send push notifications to your device. It is stored in a private area.
  • Online status: whether you are online and when you were last seen, which people you chat with can see.
  • IP address: our service providers see your IP address when your phone connects to them. The app also makes small “is the internet working” checks to Google and Cloudflare servers, which see your IP address.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your GPS location or your phone contacts. The app asks for permission to use the microphone only to record voice messages you choose to send, the camera and photo library only for pictures you choose to attach, and notifications to alert you about messages and listings. You can turn any of these off in your phone settings.

2. How we use your information

  • To run Gambish: create your account, show your listings, let you chat, and send notifications.
  • To review listings before they appear, handle reports, and keep the community safe, including preventing scams, fraud and abuse.
  • To verify identity when you apply for the Verified badge.
  • To process payments for paid features and keep the records we need.
  • To measure and fix problems, and to improve the app.
  • To follow the law and respond to valid legal requests.

3. What other people can see

  • Everyone using Gambish: your username, photo, bio, location text, website, ratings and reviews, Verified or Top Seller badges, online status, and your listings.
  • People you chat with: the messages and media you send them.
  • Gambish administrators: may view listings, reports and the content connected to them, and – only when reviewing a verification request – your ID photos. Every time an administrator opens ID photos, it is logged.

Your email address, notification token, ID photos and saved payment number are not shown to other users.

5. Where your information is stored

Our providers run servers in several countries. For example, our database is hosted in South Africa, some server functions run in the United States, and media is delivered through Cloudinary’s global network. This means your information may be processed outside The Gambia. We choose providers that protect data with industry-standard safeguards.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile and listings in the app at any time.
  • You can switch off notifications, the microphone, camera and photo access in your phone settings.
  • You can turn off auto-renew for a Boost in the app.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email support@gambishapp.com from the email address on your account. We aim to answer within 30 days.
  • You can object to how we use your information, or complain to the data-protection authority in The Gambia, if you believe we have mishandled your data.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Settings → Delete Account. If you cannot open the app, follow the steps on our Delete your account page. When your account is deleted we remove your profile, private data, listings and their photos and videos, notifications, reviews you wrote, your followers, follows and blocks, and the conversations you took part in (which means the other person will no longer see those conversations). We keep the records described in section 6 where we have a safety or legal reason to.

9. Children

Gambish is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will remove it.

10. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, access rules on our database, private storage for ID photos and payment numbers, and limited administrator access. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make an important change we will tell you in the app. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version.
